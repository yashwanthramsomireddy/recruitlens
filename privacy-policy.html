Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

RecruitLens ("we", "us", "our") is a resume-screening application built and operated by TeamExyKings. This policy explains what data we collect, how it's used, and the third parties involved in delivering the service. It applies to the RecruitLens mobile app and its associated backend.

1. Data we collect

Account data

  • Name, email address, and phone number (where provided)
  • Account type (individual or organisation) and role (e.g. team member, domain admin)
  • Usage counters — credits balance, scans performed, plan tier

Candidate / resume data

When you upload a resume for screening, we process:

  • The text content of the resume (name, contact details, work history, education, skills, and similar information as present in the file)
  • The AI-generated analysis of that resume (score, recommendation, matched/missing skills, and similar output)

Resumes are uploaded by a recruiter or team acting under their own authority. If you are a candidate whose resume was uploaded by someone else, see Section 6.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store full card numbers or bank credentials — Razorpay handles that directly. We retain transaction records (amount, date, plan/credits purchased) for accounting purposes.

Device and usage data

Standard technical data needed to operate the app (e.g. error logs) may be collected to diagnose and fix issues.

2. How we use this data

  • To provide the core service: extracting resume text and generating an AI-assisted analysis
  • To manage your account, plan, and credit balance
  • To process payments and send related receipts
  • To send service-related emails you'd reasonably expect (e.g. interview reminders you scheduled, low-credit notices)
  • To maintain an audit log of AI usage within your organisation, visible to your domain's admins

4. AI processing

Resume analysis is performed by third-party AI models accessed through OpenRouter. The specific model used depends on your selection at the time of the scan. Resume text is sent to that model's provider to generate the analysis; we do not control how each provider's infrastructure processes that request beyond what OpenRouter's own policies specify. AI-generated scores and recommendations are decision-support tools, not a guarantee of accuracy — see our Terms of Service for details.

5. Data retention

Candidate and account data is retained for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (see Section 8). Some records (e.g. payment transaction history) may be retained longer where required for accounting or legal purposes.

6. If your resume was uploaded by someone else

If a recruiter or organisation uploaded your resume to RecruitLens as part of a hiring process, that organisation is the data controller for that submission. To request access to or deletion of your data, please contact that organisation directly, or contact us and we will assist in routing your request.

7. Your rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's privacy

RecruitLens is not directed at individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
