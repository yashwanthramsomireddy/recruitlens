RecruitLens

Scan your resumes. Decide faster.

RecruitLens reads a resume, scores it with AI, and keeps every candidate in one place for you and your team. Job seekers can also rebuild their own resume in a clean template.

Coming soon to Google Play
Android app · by TeamExyKings

What it does

AI resume screening

Upload a PDF or Word resume, choose an AI model, and get a score, a recommendation, and the skills that match or are missing.

Candidate tracker

Keep status, notes and interview details for every candidate, compare candidates side by side, and export your data to Excel on paid plans.

Built for teams

Companies get their own space with teams, seats and admin approval, so people see only what their role allows.

Resume Builder

Turn your own resume into one of 16 templates, preview it first, and save it as a PDF or a Word file.

Secure sign-in

Sign in with email or Google, and switch on two-factor authentication from Settings if you want extra protection.

Your data, your say

No ads and no tracking. You can ask for your account to be deleted from inside the app at any time.
