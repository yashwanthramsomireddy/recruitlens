Delete your RecruitLens account

Last updated: 15 October 2026

App: RecruitLens  ·  Developer: TeamExyKings

You can ask us to delete your RecruitLens account, and the data linked to it, at any time. This page explains how to ask, what happens to your data, and how long it takes.

1. How to ask

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open RecruitLens and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Read what is deleted and what stays, type DELETE, and send the request.

If your account is still waiting for approval, or you signed in with Google and have not finished setting up, the same Delete my account option is on that screen.

By email (if you cannot open the app)

Send us an email from the address your account is registered with. That is how we know the request is from you. The button below opens an email that is already filled in.

Request deletion by email

Or write to support@teamexykings.in with the subject Delete my RecruitLens account. We may reply with a question to check that it is really you.

2. What happens next

  • We complete every request within 30 days.
  • Until then your account works as normal.
  • Changed your mind? Open Settings → Account → Delete Account and tap Cancel the request. If you asked by email, write to us before it is done.
  • Once a deletion is completed it cannot be undone. You can register again later, but as a brand-new person with none of your old data.

3. What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted for everyone

  • Your sign-in, so you can no longer sign in. This includes any Google link and your two-factor set-up.
  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Your notifications and your private candidate notes.
  • Your place in any teams, and any invitation addressed to your email.
  • Any resume text still waiting in the scan queue.

Also deleted for an Individual account

  • Every resume, candidate record, analysis, note, status history and scan history you uploaded. For an Individual account all of it is yours.

If you belong to an organisation

  • The organisation owns its candidate records and activity log, so they stay with it. Your name in them is replaced with "Deleted user".
  • Your unused credits go back to the organisation's pool, and your seat is freed.

What we keep

  • Payment records (amount, date, what was bought) stay for as long as accounting and tax rules require. They have no personal details left on them.
  • A small anonymised stub of the account (name "Deleted user", no email address, no phone number) so that those payment records and the organisation's logs stay intact. It cannot be used to sign in or to identify you.
  • Our database provider overwrites its routine backups on its normal schedule, so deleted data can remain in a backup for a short time before it is overwritten.

4. If you are a Domain Admin

An account that still runs an organisation cannot be deleted, because the organisation and its members depend on it. First hand the role over to someone else (Settings → Domain Admins), or contact us if the whole organisation should close. Then ask for deletion as above.

5. Delete only some of your data

You do not have to delete your account to remove data. In the app you can delete any candidate record yourself (the bin icon in Tracker, or in the header of a candidate's page), and you can sign out at any time. Resumes processed by the Resume Builder are not kept after the result is shown on your phone.

6. Candidates whose resume was uploaded by someone else

If a recruiter or company uploaded your resume to RecruitLens, that organisation holds the record. Ask them to delete it, or write to us and we will help route your request. See our Privacy Policy.

Questions about any of this? Write to support@teamexykings.in. Our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service have the full detail.
